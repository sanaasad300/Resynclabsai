Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

ResyncLabs AI ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, phone number, and email address when you contact us, call our business line, or interact with our automated messaging systems.

How We Use Information

We use collected information solely to respond to inquiries, provide requested services, and improve our automation offerings.

Mobile Information & SMS/Calling Communications

We will not share or sell your mobile information with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.

Any mobile opt-in data collected for SMS communication is not shared with third parties for these purposes. All other categories of personal information may be shared with the below vendor categories: payment processing, customer relationship management (CRM), and workflow automation providers, solely to deliver our services to you.

If you contact us by phone or text, you may receive automated or manual text message replies related to your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency may vary. You can opt out of text communications at any time by replying STOP, and receive help by replying HELP.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Your Choices

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message. You may request removal of your information by contacting us directly.
